🏡 30+ Unique Roles 🎭 3 Factions 🦄 Original Fantasy World

Veloria has a problem.

The village of Veloria is small. Everyone knows every face. Which is exactly why the betrayal cuts so deep.

Changelings — shapeshifters who feed on trust and love — have embedded themselves in the village, wearing borrowed faces, living borrowed lives. Every night, they erase someone. Every day, the village scrambles to figure out who to blame.

The problem? They're very good at looking innocent.

"You thought you knew your neighbors. You thought this village was safe."

— The Changeling Queen, probably

Three Factions. One Village. No Mercy.

Every game, every pony plays a different role. Every role has a different agenda.

Night Phase

Deceivers scheme. Harmony investigates. Neutrals pursue their own agendas. Everyone makes their move — in the dark, in secret.

02

Day Phase

Someone is dead. Accusations fly. Alliances form and break. You have to convince the village — or deflect suspicion — before the vote.

03

The Trial

The village picks a suspect. They defend themselves. Everyone votes. Someone gets exiled — and Veloria hopes they got the right one.

The World of Veloria

The village that forgot how to trust.

Veloria is not on any map. A sheltered valley, ringed by mist-choked mountains, where ponies lived for generations in gentle harmony. They built their homes from lavender stone. They lit their streets with bioluminescent lanterns. They thought they were safe.

They were wrong.

Changelings — shapeshifters who feed on trust and love — have found the valley. They don't invade with armies. They replace. A smile you recognize. A voice you've known since foalhood. A neighbor who tucks their foal in at night. But underneath: hunger.

Every morning, somepony is gone. Every day, the survivors point hooves at each other. Was it the baker? The watchmare? The princess herself? The Changelings are patient. They're already inside.

The question isn't whether Veloria falls. The question is: which side are you on?

"You thought you knew your neighbors. You thought this village was safe. But faces lie, and the ones you love most might be the ones wearing them."

— A survivor's warning, carved on the town well

Features

Built for Depth

Every mechanic serves the story. Every system rewards skill.

🧩

34 Unique Roles

No duplicates that matter. Every role has a unique ability, passive, and win condition. Investigators, protectors, killers, tricksters, merchants, and madponies.

🎭

True Social Deduction

Information is muddled, not clear. Investigators get categories, not exact roles. Deceivers can frame, disguise, and redirect. Trust is earned, not given.

⚡

Dynamic Power Scaling

Some roles grow stronger as the game progresses. Wendigo hungers. Psychopath charges. Duelist banks. The late game is never the same as the early game.

🔄

Endless Replayability

16 players. 34 roles. Random draws every match. Faction compositions shift. Role synergies emerge. No script, no memorization — just reading ponies.

🌍

An Original World

Veloria isn't a palette swap. It's a living village with history, tragedy, and secrets. Lore drops between games. Characters have backstories. The world remembers.

🎨

Pony-Style Art

Every character is a pony. Every portrait is hand-crafted. No humans, no generics — just a village of colorful, expressive equines with something to hide.
